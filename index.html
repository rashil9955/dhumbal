Dhumbal Table

Dhumbal

धुम्बल

Five cards each. Lowest total wins the round. Call Show when you think nobody can beat you.

How many at the table?

A wrong Show costs your hand total plus 25. The player who caught you scores 0.

A turn, in order

  1. Throw one card, a set of the same rank (pair, triple, four), or a run of 3+ in one suit.
  2. Take one card: the top of the deck, or a card from the previous player's throw. From a run you may take only an end card.
  3. Or call Show at the very start of your turn if you think your total is the lowest.

    Table talk

      Tips to win

      Dhumbal rewards patience more than luck. Shed weight fast, take cheap cards when they're offered, and watch who is quietly getting low.

      Throwing

      • Dump kings, queens and jacks first. They cost 11 to 13 each, and every turn you hold one is a turn you can't Show.
      • Throw in groups. A pair of 9s clears 18 points in one turn, a single only 9. Hold matching cards until you can throw them together.
      • Keep low pairs and near-runs. A 4♥ and 5♥ only need a 3♥ or 6♥ to become a run you can drop in one throw.

      Picking up

      • Take an ace, 2 or 3 from the throw rather than drawing blind. The deck averages about 7 points a card.
      • Take a card that matches one you hold, even a middling one. It turns two throws into one.
      • Don't feed your neighbour. The next player can take from your throw, so avoid throwing low singles they'd love to grab.

      Reading the table

      • Watch what others take. Someone who picks a low card from the throw is getting close to a Show.
      • Count their cards. A player down to 1 or 2 cards may be sitting on 3 points. Be careful calling Show against them.
      • Remember what was thrown. If three 5s are gone, your lone 5 will never become a set.

      Calling Show

      • 5 or less is usually safe; 8 to 10 is a gamble, especially with four or more opponents.
      • Call before others catch up. If everyone still holds 4 or 5 cards, a total of 7 often wins.
      • Near elimination, play safe. A caught Show adds 25 on top of your hand and can knock you out in one round.
